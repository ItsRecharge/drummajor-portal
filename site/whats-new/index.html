What's new

Four changes to the portal this fall: a public calendar, automatic event emails, attendance, and absence appeals. Below is what each one does and what you need to do.

What to do Read the emails
1 of 5

Public calendar

A page anyone can open without logging in. It lists every band event and can be subscribed to.

  • Address: your portal's address plus /calendar. Share it with students and parents.
  • Subscribe: the Subscribe (.ics) button gives a feed for Google Calendar (Other calendars → + → From URL) or Apple Calendar (File → New Calendar Subscription). New events show up on their phone within a few hours.
  • Automatic: anything added under Band Events is on the calendar as soon as it's saved. Drum-major-only events are not shown.
  • Linked from every email: reminders and the monthly overview end with a See the full calendar button.
2 of 5

Event emails

You add the event and pick who's expected. The portal sends the emails.

  • Who's expected: each band event names a class list (Jazz Band, Concert/Marching Band or Everyone). That decides who gets the emails and pre-fills the attendance sheet.
  • Adding an event only sends an email if the event is within a week. Anything further out just goes on the calendar.
  • Reminders go out at 9 AM one week before, three days before, and the morning of.
  • Monthly overview: on the 1st, everyone gets a list of that month's events (only if something is more than a week away).
  • Everything that goes out is listed under Email Announcements → Sent, with open counts.

3 of 5

Attendance

Take roll from the event's page. Season totals are kept for you.

  • Each band event has an Attendance button. The sheet opens with the event's class list; you can switch lists at the top.
  • Everyone starts as Absent. Tap Present, Late or Excused as you call names, or Mark all present and fix the exceptions. The search box finds a name.
  • You can edit it later. Re-saving updates the sheet; the event row shows a Taken badge.
  • Season totals are under Attendance in the sidebar: expected, present, late, excused, absent, and a rate that ignores excused absences. Both screens have a CSV export.

The Attendance page

Pending appeals are at the top. Below that is the season table for the class list you pick.

4 of 5

Absences and appeals

Absent students are emailed automatically and can ask for the absence to be excused.

  • 30 minutes after you save, every student still marked Absent gets an email saying attendance is mandatory and may impact their grade, with a personal Appeal this absence link. The 30 minutes gives you time to fix mistakes; re-saving restarts the clock, and nobody is emailed twice for the same event.
  • The appeal is one text box, no login needed. When a student sends one, you get a notification and an email.
  • Excuse it or Deny on the Attendance page. Excuse it changes their record to Excused. Either way the student gets an email with the decision.
  • Fixing the sheet also works. If you change someone from Absent to Present yourself, their pending appeal is approved automatically and they are emailed.

Who is named in the rule

Settings → Attendance policy has the conflict contact (Mr. Costello, editable) and a drum major picked from the portal to CC. Their name and email address go into every email.

If that drum major graduates or is removed, the next leader who signs in sees a banner asking them to pick a replacement.

Checklist

What to do

Five things to keep doing.

1

Add every event as soon as you know it

Title, date, time, place, and who's expected. Events far out don't send an email; they just appear on the calendar and in the monthly overview.

2

Share the calendar address

Your portal address + /calendar. Tell people to use Subscribe so it shows up on their phone.

3

Take attendance at every rehearsal and performance

Open the event → Attendance → tap names → Save. You have 30 minutes to fix mistakes before absence emails go out.

4

Check the Attendance page for appeals

You get a notification and an email when one comes in. Excuse it or Deny; the student is emailed either way.

5

Keep the CC drum major current

If a banner asks who should be CC'd on conflicts, pick someone. It means the previous person is no longer in the portal.
